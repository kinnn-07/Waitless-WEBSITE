# WaitLess: 3-minute demo

1. **Landing page (0:00–0:25):** Show the four-step flow, College office / Clinic / Salon tiles, and display-only Free, Starter, and Pro pricing.
2. **Customer queue (0:25–1:00):** Open CarePlus Clinic, choose a service, enter a demo name and 10-digit phone number, and join. Point out the large token, people ahead, estimated wait, privacy note, and leave option.
3. **Business dashboard (1:00–1:45):** Sign in with `admin@waitless.demo` / `waitless123`, open CarePlus, and call next. Start serving and mark the visit done. Keep a token page open in another tab to show the live update; the approaching-turn banner appears when the customer is two places away.
4. **Queue operations (1:45–2:20):** Add a walk-in, pause and reopen the queue, and start **Simulate customers**. Show service editing and working-hour controls, then stop simulation.
5. **Booking and QR tools (2:20–3:00):** Book an available appointment slot, show it disappear from the available slots, then cancel it. Open the A4 QR poster and full-screen public display for the same location.

All data stays in browser storage and synchronizes across open tabs. No real accounts, notifications, or customer data are required for the demo.
